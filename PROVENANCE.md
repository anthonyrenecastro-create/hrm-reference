It records the licensor's personal attestation but does not replace a legal ownership review.

- The repository was created specifically for Anthony Castro from those materials.

## Automated review performed

Before public release, the tracked files are checked for:

- residual Apache license declarations outside the historical changelog statement;
- GPL, AGPL, LGPL, proprietary-license, or third-party copyright markers;
- untracked or vendored source trees;
- consistency among `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `README.md`, `pyproject.toml`, and `CITATION.cff`;
- package metadata containing the SPDX expression `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`.
